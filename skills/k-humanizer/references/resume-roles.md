# Field-Aware Resume Writing

Read this reference after `resume.md` when the source includes a target role,
job description, or clear professional context. Its first job is to keep the
field's real language and work intact while removing AI-like wording. Change
the order of verified experience only when the user asks for tailoring. Never
change the facts, contribution boundary, measurement conditions, or completion
state.

## Use field context safely

- Use the field context to understand established terms and what the work means.
- For a humanization-only request, keep the original experience selection and
  order.
- If the user asks for job targeting and the posting combines roles, choose one
  primary role and at most one supporting role.
- If no target role is supplied, do not infer one. Apply the general rules in
  `resume.md`.
- Do not copy the posting's responsibilities into the resume as experience.
- Keep a real gap visible when the posting asks for work the source does not
  support.

## Field context table

| Role | Experience details to preserve | Evidence that helps | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operations | Repeated work, exceptions, deadlines, handoffs, and operating decisions | Volume, frequency, time range, error type, turnaround time, escalation rule, before/after observation | `운영 전반`, `원활한 운영`, `효율성 향상` without the changed routine or result |
| Service or product planning | User or business problem, option considered, priority, scope decision, and validation | Inquiry or behavior pattern, requirement, tradeoff, policy, launch state, experiment or feedback result | `기획에 참여`, `니즈를 반영`, `방향성을 수립` without the actual decision |
| QA | Test scope, environment, reproduction condition, severity, release decision, and follow-up | Device or scenario coverage, failure condition, defect priority, blocked release, fix verification, recurrence check | Test-case count alone, `품질을 강화`, or a defect claimed from a check that found none |
| Design | User task, information hierarchy, constraint, design decision, and validation | Research finding, prototype, usability task, design system rule, handoff scope, observed task result | `감각적인`, `트렌디한`, `사용자 경험 개선` without the design decision or evidence |
| Marketing or content | Audience, channel, hypothesis, message, distribution, and measured response | Reach condition, sample, open or click rate, conversion boundary, editorial decision, attribution source | `바이럴`, `브랜드 강화`, `성과 창출` without a measured response or source |
| Customer service or sales | Customer problem, policy, response, follow-up, and confirmed outcome | Inquiry type, case volume, response time, escalation, retention or satisfaction measurement condition | `친절한 응대`, `고객 만족 향상`, or company sales presented as individual impact |
| Research or data | Question, method, sample, limitation, finding, and decision supported | Data source, period, sample, comparison, coding or analysis method, uncertainty | `인사이트 도출`, `객관적 분석`, or correlation presented as causation |
| People operations or education | Learner or team need, program decision, delivery, participation, and follow-up | Audience size, session count, completion, internal survey, material ownership, later adoption state | `역량 강화`, `성장 지원`, or company-wide adoption inferred from a pilot |

## What a strong bullet answers

### Operations

1. What repeated work or exception made operations difficult?
2. What rule, schedule, handoff, or response did the person change?
3. What volume, time, error, or follow-up result was confirmed?

Before:

> 운영 전반을 책임지며 원활한 프로세스를 구축해 효율성을 향상시켰습니다.

After, when the source supports the details:

> 월 1,200건의 주문에서 주소 오류를 별도로 분류하고 출고 전 확인 순서를 정리해 재확인 요청 비율을 18%에서 7%로 낮췄습니다.

### Service or product planning

1. What user or business problem was observed?
2. What requirement, priority, policy, or scope did the person decide?
3. Was it proposed, approved, launched, or validated?

Before:

> 사용자 니즈를 분석해 서비스 개선 방향성을 수립했습니다.

After, when the source supports the details:

> 취소 문의 240건을 유형별로 나누고 반복 문의가 많았던 환불 조건을 신청 화면에 먼저 보여 주도록 정책과 노출 순서를 정했습니다.

### QA

1. What feature, environment, and scenario were tested?
2. What exact condition reproduced the failure?
3. How did the finding affect priority, release, or later verification?

Before:

> 다양한 테스트 케이스를 수행해 서비스 품질 강화에 기여했습니다.

After, when the source supports the details:

> 결제 취소 흐름을 6개 환경과 48개 시나리오에서 확인하고 중복 환불로 이어지는 조건 3건을 출시 전에 재현해 수정 후 같은 조건으로 다시 검증했습니다.

### Design

1. What task was difficult for the user?
2. What constraint and design decision changed the experience?
3. What prototype, research, or observed task result supports it?

Before:

> 감각적이고 트렌디한 UI를 설계해 사용자 경험을 개선했습니다.

After, when the source supports the details:

> 예약 완료까지 다섯 단계였던 흐름을 세 단계로 줄인 프로토타입을 만들고 사용자 8명에게 확인해 과업 완료 인원을 6명에서 8명으로 높였습니다.

## Shared safeguards

- A role keyword provides context; it does not prove the experience.
- Counts need meaning. `테스트 127건`, `콘텐츠 40건`, or `문의 800건` is
  activity until the source connects it to scope, judgment, or result.
- Keep proposal, approval, pilot, launch, and organization-wide adoption
  separate.
- Keep team outcomes and personal ownership separate.
- For qualitative work, a clear decision and method can be strong evidence even
  without a percentage. Do not fabricate a metric to make the role look mature.
- Preserve exact domain language when recruiters need it, but explain unfamiliar
  internal wording through the actual task or decision.

## Final role check

- The wording still describes the field's actual work instead of replacing it
  with generic achievement language.
- When tailoring was requested, the first two bullets answer the target role's
  main hiring questions.
- Each section contributes different information instead of repeating one claim.
- The wording shows judgment and responsibility, not only task lists.
- Weak or missing experience remains visible instead of being covered by the
  posting's keywords.
- A hiring manager from the target field can explain what the person actually
  did, under which conditions, and what was confirmed.
